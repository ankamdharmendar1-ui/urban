About Urbanlive

Urbanlive.fr is a free online anime streaming index that allows fans to watch their favourite anime series and movies online in HD quality — with both English subtitles (Sub) and English dubbing (Dub) available.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make anime accessible to everyone, everywhere. We believe that great stories should be available to all fans, regardless of geography or budget.

What We Offer

We aggregate and index anime content from across the web, providing a clean and user-friendly experience so you can find and watch your favourite shows without hassle. Our library includes thousands of anime titles — from classic series to the latest seasonal releases.

How It Works

Urbanlive.fr does not host any video files directly. We index and embed video streams provided by third-party hosting services. All content is served from external servers; we simply provide an organized way to access it.

Community

We are passionate anime fans ourselves. Our site is built for the community, and we continually work to improve the experience based on user feedback. You can request anime titles, report issues, and engage through our contact and request features.
