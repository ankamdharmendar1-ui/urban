DMCA Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Urbanlive.fr respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects our users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), we will respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement.

Our Position

Urbanlive.fr does not host, upload, or store any video content on its servers. All video streams are provided by third-party hosting services. We act solely as an index/aggregator of links to content hosted elsewhere.

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been linked on our site in a way that constitutes copyright infringement, please send a written notice to our designated agent containing the following:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or an authorized agent.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, with enough information to locate it.
  • Your contact information (address, phone number, and email).
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Send DMCA Notices To

Please contact us via our contact page with your DMCA notice. We will review and act promptly.

Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, you may submit a counter-notice. Please contact us with the details and we will review your request.
